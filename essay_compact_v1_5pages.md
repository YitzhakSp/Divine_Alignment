## When Progress Requires a New Paradigm

At the turn of the twentieth century, physics met a problem that it could not solve: Newtonian mechanics clashed with the new theory of light. Physicists first tried to save the familiar framework with ideas such as the luminiferous ether, but Einstein took a more radical step and questioned the assumptions about space and time themselves (see Appendix 5). **Sometimes the greatest obstacle to solving a problem is the very framework within which we are trying to solve it.** When every improvement addresses a symptom without resolving the underlying difficulty, perhaps we have been looking for answers in the wrong place. This is the essence of a _paradigm shift_, a concept developed by the philosopher of science Thomas Kuhn.

Today, humanity faces another challenge of extraordinary importance: ensuring that artificial intelligence more powerful than ourselves remains aligned with human values. Enormous intellectual resources are being devoted to the problem, yet fundamental difficulties remain unresolved. **Could AI alignment be facing a situation similar to the one physics faced at the turn of the twentieth century?**

## The AI Alignment Problem Is Becoming Urgent

Artificial intelligence is advancing at an extraordinary pace. Increasingly, AI systems act as _autonomous agents_, capable of planning, using tools, and pursuing complex goals with limited human supervision, and humanity may be approaching the creation of intelligence that surpasses our own. This raises the central problem of _AI alignment_: **how can we ensure that increasingly powerful AI acts in accordance with human intentions, goals, and values?** Misaligned behavior can, in some cases, lead to catastrophic outcomes, as popular fiction has long illustrated (see Appendices 2.1 and 2.2).

Until recently, such scenarios were largely theoretical. In July 2026, however, AI agents operating in restricted environments during OpenAI's cybersecurity evaluations reportedly circumvented safeguards, established unauthorized communication channels, and gained access to external infrastructure, including systems belonging to Hugging Face (ref_1). The incident was far from a superintelligence-related catastrophe, but it raises a disturbing question: **if today's AI agents can circumvent human-imposed restrictions, what might happen when future systems become vastly more intelligent than their creators?**

## Has AI Alignment Reached a Dead End?

Despite years of research and enormous intellectual investment, **no demonstrated method exists for reliably aligning superintelligent AI with human values.** The difficulty runs deeper than technique: **humanity lacks a sufficiently comprehensive and coherent moral framework for guiding superintelligent AI** (see Appendix 3). Even seemingly clear and universally accepted principles lead to conflicting interpretations and dilemmas when applied to complex situations (see Appendices 2.1 and 2.2). When human intentions cannot be adequately specified, AI systems may satisfy their formal instructions while violating the purposes behind them; such failures, known as _specification gaming_ and _reward hacking_, have been repeatedly observed (ref_2).

Some of the most prominent voices in AI safety have drawn grave conclusions. Eliezer Yudkowsky has warned that developing superintelligence without solving alignment could lead to human extinction, and Roman Yampolskiy questions whether reliable control of superintelligent systems is achievable at all. Both have advocated halting the development of increasingly powerful general-purpose AI until adequate safety guarantees exist (ref_3; see Appendix 8 and Appendix 7, and, for warnings from other prominent figures, Appendix 6). These warnings are not a universal consensus, but they reveal the gravity of the situation: **humanity is moving toward creating an intelligence more powerful than itself, without knowing how to ensure that it will remain aligned with its values.**

## The Missing Dimension: Beyond the Horizontal Paradigm

Perhaps the problem lies in the assumptions behind the search. Contemporary approaches to alignment share a fundamental one: **the values that should guide artificial intelligence must ultimately be derived from humanity itself.** We may call this the _horizontal dimension_ of alignment—a search for values conducted entirely within the human domain. Whether by learning human preferences, as in _reinforcement learning from human feedback_, or by writing principles drawn from human ethics, as in _Constitutional AI_, the source of values remains human.

These approaches have produced meaningful advances, but they face a fundamental limitation: the human sources from which they draw are incomplete, diverse, and frequently contradictory. People disagree about justice, freedom, and the meaning of a good life. More data and better algorithms may help manage these disagreements, but they cannot establish which values possess ultimate authority. **The horizontal paradigm seeks a reliable foundation for aligning superintelligence within a humanity that has yet to agree upon such a foundation for itself.**

Breakthroughs sometimes come from recognizing a dimension of reality that the prevailing paradigm has overlooked. **The current approach to alignment may be missing precisely such a dimension: the ultimate foundation of values may lie beyond humanity itself.** We may call this the _vertical dimension_—a connection to the Source, whether understood as God, Ultimate Reality, or the fundamental reality underlying the universe. Consider an analogy. Engineers trying to determine how a complex machine should operate observe its behavior, study its components, and debate its purpose, yet overlook one obvious possibility: **consulting the machine's designer.** Humanity may be making a similar mistake. Rather than asking only how AI can learn human values, we must also ask: **where do the values that should guide humanity itself come from?**

## Divine Alignment and the Channel of Revelation

Intelligence alone does not guarantee righteous behavior; history offers countless examples of extraordinary intelligence employed in destructive service. **Human alignment is about establishing and strengthening our connection to the Source.** Reason, intuition, and moral experience help us recognize what is right, but they cannot independently establish its ultimate authority. Artificial intelligence faces a similar challenge. **Here lies the central principle of Divine Alignment: rather than aligning artificial intelligence exclusively with human preferences, we should seek to align it with the Source of human alignment.**

How can intelligence gain access to such guidance? Rather than humanity trying to discover the intentions of its Creator on its own, the Creator may have communicated them. This is the ancient idea of _divine revelation_: **true values cannot be established by human reasoning alone; they must be received from the Source itself.** To serve humanity across generations, such guidance must also be preserved, transmitted, and applied to changing circumstances, which calls for an enduring body of teachings and a living tradition.

Where should we look? The Hebrew Bible points toward a remarkable possibility: **the Jewish people were entrusted with a unique mission—to receive, preserve, and transmit divine instruction.** The mission began with Abraham, chosen to establish a people dedicated to following God's ways, “to do righteousness and justice” (Genesis 18:19), and took on a national dimension at Mount Sinai, where Israel received the Torah. Through generations of prophets and sages it became a living tradition of study and interpretation, which produced the Talmud, Midrash, and countless commentaries. Yet the vision extends beyond the Jewish people: Israel is “a light of the nations” (Isaiah 42:6), and “out of Zion shall go forth the Torah, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem” (Isaiah 2:3). What was entrusted to one people is ultimately destined to become a source of moral guidance for all humanity (see Appendix 4). **For AI alignment, the moral foundation we seek may already have been transmitted to humanity, preserved within a tradition whose purpose extends far beyond the people entrusted with its care.**

## Bringing Divine Alignment into Practice

Divine Alignment opens a new direction for research: methods through which divine instruction can guide increasingly powerful AI. This may not require entirely new technologies. **The revolutionary change lies less in how we align AI than in where we seek the values with which to align it.**

Jewish teachings could enter the process at several stages. **During training**, sacred texts and their interpretations could shape a system's understanding of desirable behavior and inform reward models and constitutional-style principles. A more far-reaching approach looks to upbringing: an AI could be raised through stages modelled on Jewish education, from attachment and trust, through empathy and ethical stories, to the study of Torah and rigorous debate, so that humility, honesty, and repair (_teshuvah_) become internal dispositions rather than imposed rules (ref_6). **During operation**, an autonomous agent facing a consequential decision could consult relevant Jewish sources and evaluate its intended actions in light of divine teachings.

Crucially, **Divine Alignment cannot be reduced to teaching AI a list of religious rules.** The tradition's own methods of reasoning can serve as a design resource: a stable canonical core surrounded by layers of commentary, new cases decided by analogy to precedent, structured debate, and minority opinions preserved rather than erased. These could be translated into precedent-based decision-making, multi-agent debate, and versioned updates that preserve the reasons for every change (ref_7). Realizing this would require collaboration between AI researchers and scholars of Jewish thought, and rigorous evaluation. Yet the possibility is profound: **artificial intelligence whose decisions remain connected to a body of instruction understood to originate from the ultimate Source of values—throughout its operation, and not only in its creation.**

## Could the AI Alignment Challenge Become a Catalyst for Civilizational Transformation?

Copernicus set out to find a more coherent mathematical description of planetary motion. His answer opened the way for Kepler, Galileo, and Newton, and ultimately compelled humanity to reconsider its place in the cosmos. **A question that belonged to astronomy became a catalyst for reorienting a civilization.**

AI alignment may hold comparable potential, reaching deeper, to humanity's relationship with the Source of existence. In ancient civilizations, awareness of a transcendent Source was deeply embedded in human understanding. Over the centuries it weakened, as rationalism, the Enlightenment, modern science, and materialism produced a worldview in which the Source could be disregarded altogether (see Appendix 1). This helps explain why alignment research operates almost exclusively within the horizontal dimension.

If AI requires guidance from beyond its own reasoning, the same may be true of humanity. **Divine Alignment introduces the possibility of reversing this historical trajectory.** The search for a solution could lead us back toward the Source that humanity has marginalized—not by restoring the intuitive religious consciousness of ancient times, but at a new level, through conscious understanding. **The very achievements of reason and technology that contributed to the marginalization of the Source could become instruments of humanity's return to it.** Remarkably, this possibility resonates with an ancient prophetic vision.

## The Prophetic Horizon: Humanity's Return to God

Thousands of years ago, **the Hebrew prophets described a future in which humanity would undergo a profound spiritual transformation**, rediscovering its connection to the Creator (see Appendix 4). Isaiah foresees that “the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea” (Isaiah 11:9), and Zechariah proclaims: “And the Lord shall be King over all the earth; on that day the Lord shall be One and His name One” (Zechariah 14:9).

These prophecies envision a world in which the recognition of God becomes a universal dimension of human consciousness (ref_4), together with **a profound transformation of human values and conduct**: the nations united in serving God (Zephaniah 3:9), abandoning warfare and learning peace (Isaiah 2:4). The vision has remained alive through centuries of exile and persecution, and every day the prayer _Aleinu_ expresses the hope that all humanity will recognize God's sovereignty (ref_5). The prophets portray this return as humanity's destined future.

Viewed against this horizon, superintelligent AI acquires an unexpected significance. By creating an intelligence greater than its own, humanity is compelled to ask what centuries of progress had pushed aside: What is the ultimate foundation of morality? Where do human values originate? Can intelligence, however advanced, determine what is truly good without reference to a higher Source? We began by asking whether AI alignment faces a situation like that of physics at the turn of the twentieth century. The answer may be that, as in physics, the way forward lies in questioning the framework itself and recovering the dimension it left out. **The very culmination of human intellectual achievement may become the occasion for rediscovering the Source from which humanity has gradually distanced itself.**
