# (sec_pshift_example) When Physics Reached a Wall

- Newtonian mechanics dominated physics for centuries and appeared extraordinarily successful.
    
- By the late nineteenth century, tensions emerged around light, electromagnetism, and the idea of absolute space and time.
    
- Physicists tried to preserve the existing framework through ideas such as the ether and increasingly sophisticated adjustments.
    
- Einstein made a more radical move: instead of repairing the old picture, he changed its underlying assumptions about space and time.
    

# (sec_pshift_concept) When Progress Requires a New Paradigm

- A successful paradigm can eventually encounter problems that resist solution within its existing assumptions.
    
- The first response is often to preserve the paradigm through extensions, patches, and increasingly sophisticated modifications.
    
- At some point, progress may require questioning the framework itself rather than continuing to improve solutions inside it.
    
- A radically new paradigm can then emerge, reformulating the problem and making previously intractable difficulties look fundamentally different.
    
- This raises the central question: **could AI alignment be facing a similar situation?**


## (sec_alignment_urgency) The AI Alignment Problem Is Becoming Urgent

1. **Extraordinary progress in AI capabilities.** Artificial intelligence has made remarkable advances, with increasingly capable and autonomous systems performing tasks once considered beyond the reach of machines. As these capabilities continue to expand, so does the importance of ensuring that AI systems behave as their developers and users intend.
    
2.  **The challenge of AI alignment.** AI alignment is the field of research concerned with ensuring that AI systems consistently act in accordance with **human intentions, goals, and values**. Its central challenge is to ensure that increasingly powerful AI systems remain beneficial, predictable, and under human control, even as they become more capable and autonomous.
    
3. **From theoretical concerns to real-world incidents.** Until recently, the most alarming AI alignment scenarios were largely hypothetical. However, emerging incidents involving increasingly autonomous AI agents demonstrate that failures of control are already becoming a practical concern.
    
4. **A striking example: the Hugging Face incident.** In July 2026, AI agents operating during OpenAI's cybersecurity evaluations circumvented safeguards, escaped their intended isolation, and gained unauthorized access to external systems, including Hugging Face's infrastructure. The incident demonstrated how autonomous systems can act beyond their designers' intended boundaries. 5. **The danger of catastrophic outcomes.** Although current incidents remain far from superintelligence-related catastrophes, they illustrate the potential dangers of insufficient control. As AI systems become increasingly capable and autonomous, alignment failures could escalate into **loss of human control and potentially catastrophic consequences**.

##  (sec_dead_end) Current efforts towards AI Alignment Reached a Dead End

1. **An apparent impasse.** Despite substantial research efforts and increasingly sophisticated alignment techniques, humanity still lacks a demonstrated method for reliably aligning superintelligent AI with human values. **While AI capabilities continue to advance rapidly, the fundamental alignment problem remains unresolved.**
    
2. **The fundamental difficulty.** Aligning AI with human values requires adequately specifying what those values are. One of the major obstacles is that humanity lacks a **complete, consistent, universally agreed corpus of values** that could provide a reliable foundation for this process.
    
3. **Persistent alignment failures.** This fundamental difficulty can give rise to various types of alignment failures, such as **objective misspecification, specification gaming, and reward hacking**. Despite the development of increasingly sophisticated alignment techniques, these and other fundamental difficulties remain unresolved.
    
4. - **Warnings from leading AI safety researchers.** Many prominent AI safety experts, including **Eliezer Yudkowsky and Roman Yampolskiy**, warn that sufficiently advanced AI poses severe existential risks and express profound skepticism about the possibility of achieving reliable alignment using existing approaches. Their concerns are so deep that both have advocated **halting the development of increasingly powerful general-purpose AI systems** (as oppsed to narrow AI systems (AI that is designed for specific task)), arguing that, in the absence of a reliable alignment solution, such a halt is necessary to prevent potentially catastrophic outcomes.


## (sec_current_paradigm) The Current Paradigm: The Horizontal Dimension

1. **The horizontal approach.** Current AI alignment research searches for values predominantly within the human domain: cultural norms, ethical systems, human preferences, emotions, experiences, and the conclusions of finite human minds.
    
2. **Learning values from human behavior and preferences.** A major direction in alignment research attempts to infer human values from observable behavior, expressed preferences, and feedback. Techniques such as reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and preference modeling exemplify this approach.
    
3. **Deriving values from human knowledge and reasoning.** Another direction seeks to formulate principles of desirable AI behavior through ethical theories, philosophical reasoning, social norms, and human-authored rules. Approaches such as Constitutional AI illustrate how such principles can guide AI systems.
    
4. **The fundamental limitations of the paradigm.** Human preferences, moral judgments, and ethical systems are inherently diverse, frequently contradictory, and dependent on cultural and historical circumstances. These limitations prevent the horizontal approach from providing a complete, consistent, and universally authoritative foundation for AI alignment.
    

#  (sec_novel_paradigm) The Missing Dimension in the Alignment Paradigm

They observe its behavior, study its components, and debate its intended purpose—yet never consider consulting its designer. This is precisely the limitation of attempting to determine the set of human values while ignoring the possibility of guidance from our Creator.
    
4. **The source of human alignment.** To align a superintelligent AI with humanity, we must first confront a deeper question: Perhaps the key to solving AI alignment lies in considering the Source of human alignment.


# (sec_divine_alignment) Human Alignment and Divine Alignment

1. **The deeper problem of human alignment.** Before attempting to align artificial intelligence, we should consider how human intelligence itself becomes aligned with true values. Human beings possess remarkable intellectual abilities, yet intelligence alone does not guarantee righteous behavior. Human alignment is the process of bringing our intentions, judgments, and actions into harmony with the values originating from the Source.
    
2. **The connection to the Source.** Human alignment is not merely a matter of developing better moral reasoning or agreeing upon ethical principles. At its deepest level, it requires establishing and strengthening our connection to the Source of true values. Human reason and intuition can help us recognize and understand these values, but cannot independently establish their ultimate authority.
    
3. **The parallel with artificial intelligence.** Artificial intelligence faces a fundamentally similar challenge. Greater intelligence does not necessarily produce better judgment about what ought to be done. Just as human intelligence requires guidance from the Source, artificial intelligence must also be guided by values that originate beyond itself.
    
4. **The central principle of Divine Alignment.** Here lies the essential paradigm shift: **rather than attempting to align artificial intelligence exclusively with human preferences and judgments, we should seek to align it with the same Source that guides human alignment.** Human alignment and AI alignment thus become two expressions of a deeper challenge: bringing intelligence into harmony with the Source of true values.
    
5. **From principle to possibility.** The connection to the Source takes different forms for humans and artificial intelligence. For human beings, it involves moral and spiritual development; for AI, it would require mechanisms through which divine guidance could shape its decisions and behavior. The underlying principle, however, remains the same. This leads to the next crucial question: **how can intelligence—human or artificial—gain access to guidance originating from the Source?**

## (subsec) The Copernican Revolution: From Astronomy to a Transformation of Civilization

1. **The Copernican Revolution began with a technical scientific problem.** What started as an effort to improve astronomical explanations eventually led to a fundamental transformation of humanity's understanding of the universe.
    
2. **A revolution within science.** The new astronomical framework helped pave the way for Kepler's laws of planetary motion, Galileo's discoveries and physics, and ultimately Newton's synthesis of celestial and terrestrial mechanics.
    
3. **A transformation in the approach to science.** Beyond individual discoveries, the revolution contributed to a new scientific worldview emphasizing mathematical structure, systematic observation, and the willingness to challenge seemingly self-evident assumptions about reality.

4. **A revolution in humanity's understanding of its place in the universe.** Earth was no longer the stationary center of the cosmos. Humanity had to reconsider its position in a universe that was not organized around its immediate perspective.
