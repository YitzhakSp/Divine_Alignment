
# The Prophetic Horizon: Humanity’s Return to God

The preceding section suggested that the challenge of AI alignment might become a catalyst for humanity's return to divine guidance. Remarkably, such a transformation is not merely a modern philosophical possibility. Thousands of years ago, the Hebrew prophets described a future in which humanity would undergo a profound spiritual transformation, rediscovering its connection to the Creator and recognizing God as the ultimate Source of truth, morality, and guidance.

 The prophetic vision extends far beyond the Jewish people to encompass all humanity. The prophet Isaiah declares:

> _“For the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea.”_ (Isaiah 11:9; cf. Habakkuk 2:14)

This vision appears repeatedly throughout the prophetic writings. Zechariah proclaims: _“And the Lord shall be King over all the earth; on that day the Lord shall be One and His name One”_ (Zechariah 14:9). Isaiah further describes a time when the divine presence will become manifest to all humanity: _“And the glory of the Lord shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see it together”_ (Isaiah 40:5). These prophecies envision a world fundamentally different from our own—one in which the recognition of God is no longer confined to particular nations, traditions, or individuals, but becomes a universal dimension of human consciousness. The prophets describe far more than humanity's intellectual acknowledgment of God's existence. They envision a profound transformation of human values, priorities, and conduct. Zephaniah (3:9) foresees the nations united in serving God, while Isaiah (2:4) describes a world in which nations abandon warfare and learn peace. In this vision, humanity does not merely discover a higher truth; it reorganizes civilization around that truth. Divine guidance becomes the foundation of human relationships, moral decisions, and collective life.

**A vision preserved through millennia.** These prophecies have never been merely ancient predictions preserved in sacred texts. Their message has remained alive in Jewish consciousness throughout centuries of exile, persecution, and historical upheaval. Every day, in the concluding prayer _Aleinu Leshabeach_, Jews express their hope for a world in which all humanity recognizes God's sovereignty. The prayer culminates with Zechariah's declaration that God will become King over the entire earth. What the prophets envisioned thousands of years ago has thus remained a living aspiration, passed from generation to generation. [Chabad](https://www.chabad.org/library/article_cdo/aid/7424392/jewish/Aleinu-Text-with-Translation.htm?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**The prophetic promise.** Perhaps the most remarkable feature of these prophecies is their certainty. The prophets do not present humanity's spiritual transformation merely as an attractive ideal or a possibility dependent upon favorable historical circumstances. They proclaim it as humanity's destined future. Despite the conflicts, moral failures, and spiritual confusion that characterize human history, the prophetic vision points toward an ultimate reconciliation between humanity and its Creator. The return to the Source is not simply one possible direction of human development; it is portrayed as the culmination of humanity's historical journey.

**Divine Alignment and the fulfillment of an ancient vision.** Viewed against this prophetic horizon, the emergence of superintelligent AI acquires an unexpected significance. Humanity's attempt to create an intelligence surpassing its own may compel it to confront questions that centuries of scientific and technological progress have increasingly pushed aside: What is the ultimate foundation of morality? Where do human values originate? And can intelligence, however advanced, determine what is truly good without reference to a higher Source?

As mentioned previously, the challenge of aligning artificial intelligence may thus become a catalyst for humanity's own transformation. A technological endeavor aimed at ensuring that machines follow human values could ultimately lead humanity to reconsider the foundations of those values and rediscover its relationship with the Creator.

In this perspective, the history of humanity acquires a remarkable coherence. From its original awareness of the transcendent Source, through a long process of spiritual separation and increasing reliance on human reason, humanity may now be approaching a turning point. **The very culmination of human intellectual achievement—the creation of an intelligence greater than our own—may become the occasion for rediscovering the Source from which humanity has gradually distanced itself.**
