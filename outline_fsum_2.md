## Compared with outline_fsum_1.md

**Strengths**
- It keeps the essay's signature dynamic and is the most faithful to the dynamics of the progression.
- Dropping the headings saves about 10 lines, so it can carry about 40 more words.
- It reads as one argument. The first outline reads as five blocks.

**Weaknesses**
- It is harder to scan. With no headings, the bold questions carry all the structure.
- Paragraphs 4 and 5 are only about 100 words each, so they are thin on the "why". The revelation step could sound like an assertion.

## Shared with outline_fsum_1.md

- Both drop ref_2, ref_5, the *Aleinu* sentence and the Copernicus analogy.
- Both leave the same open decisions: whether to renumber the references 1 to 5 or keep the old numbers (ref_1, 3, 4, 6, 7), and whether to keep the appendix pointers in the text or replace them with one closing line.
- All nine appendices stay cited in both. In this outline Appendix 4 appears once (paragraph 5), and Appendices 2.1 and 2.2 are cited once. In the first outline Appendix 4 appears twice.

## Recommendation

Use this version for the 2-page text, because it makes the cuts feel like compression and not omission.
